Dear Mama,

There are no words in any language that could truly capture what you mean to me. You have been my first home, my safest place, my loudest cheerleader, and my quietest comfort.

You taught me what love truly looks like: not the easy kind, but the kind that shows up tired, the kind that gives even when there is nothing left. That is you.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for choosing me, every day.

Your child, always 💛
